### Hi, I am Li Wei

I design and build software, embedded systems, and electronic products. And yep, my name is Li Wei, not Wei Li. If you do not respect my cultural diversity, I will burn your house down.

My work spans from low-level systems and firmware to developer tools, engineering software, and IoT devices. I tend to work across the boundaries between disciplines rather than staying within a single technology stack.

## Open source projects

* [EdgeLinkd](https://github.com/oldrev/edgelinkd)
  A Rust implementation of a Node-RED compatible flow-based programming environment.
* [BorneoIoT](https://github.com/borneo-iot/borneo)
  An open hardware and software platform for aquarium LED, built around ESP32, embedded firmware, local networking, and Flutter app.
* [MaltReport](https://github.com/oldrev/maltreport)
  A .NET document generation and templating system for producing structured office documents and reports.
* [MapMatchingKit](https://github.com/oldrev/mapmatchingkit)
  A C# adaptation of Barefoot for GPS map matching and road-network processing.
* [TIDS](https://github.com/oldrev/tids)
  A Typst template for producing engineering datasheets for electronic components.
* [Branchie](https://github.com/oldrev/branchie)
  An HTML5 interactive video player based on finite-state-machine concepts.
* [LWDT](https://github.com/oldrev/lwdt)
  A lightweight device-tree and driver framework solution for ESP-IDF.

## Current Work

I am currently developing several private and experimental projects around engineering software and electronic systems.

One of them is VibEDA, a PCB-level EDA built from scratch with .NET and Avalonia. 
It explores how PCB design software can represent engineering objects, geometry, connectivity, and design intent in a form suitable for AI-assisted workflows.

VibEDA is a private project.
